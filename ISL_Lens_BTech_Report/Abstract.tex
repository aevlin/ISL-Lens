\chapter*{ABSTRACT}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{ABSTRACT} % to add abstract to table of contents
\begin{spacing}{1.15}
Over 18 million Deaf and Hard-of-Hearing (D/HOH) individuals across India face severe systemic communication barriers in healthcare, legal proceedings, education, and daily commercial transactions. This crisis is exacerbated by a severe national shortage of certified Indian Sign Language (ISL) interpreters, of whom fewer than 300 operate nationwide. Prior automated sign language recognition systems have primarily relied either on cumbersome, expensive wearable sensor gloves ($\geq$ \textdollar 200) that impede natural dexterity, or on resource-heavy 3D Convolutional Neural Networks requiring enterprise GPUs, exhibiting high latency ($>200$\,ms) and lighting fragility. Furthermore, existing research rarely addresses spatial body-region ambiguity, continuous Subject-Object-Verb (SOV) grammatical translation, or on-device vocabulary customization for end users.

To overcome these limitations, this project presents ISL-Lens, an edge-first, zero-hardware, browser-native real-time Indian Sign Language recognition console, continuous sentence interpreter, and interactive sign practice \& AI retraining studio. Operating through standard webcams via HTML5 and WebRTC, the framework leverages Google MediaPipe Holistic tracking to extract 828 3D spatial coordinates across 75 hand and torso keypoints at 30+ FPS. A spatial-temporal pipeline computes torso-normalized coordinates, 12 anatomical body-region spatial anchors (differentiating signs articulated near the temple, forehead, nose, mouth, chin, or torso), and kinematic trajectory statistics over a 3.0-second observation window. Classification is performed across an approved 100-word ISL vocabulary, coupled with a calibrated open-set rejection threshold ($\tau = 0.35$). Continuous sign streams are transformed into fluent English sentences via an automated ISL (SOV) to English (SVO) natural language generation engine with speech synthesis. Additionally, ISL-Lens introduces an in-camera \textit{Interactive Practice Studio} providing real-time circular accuracy HUD scoring (e.g., 96.5\% match), alongside an in-browser \textit{AI Retraining Studio} enabling signers to record live exemplars and retrain custom signs on-device, protected by an automated $\ge 90\%$ held-out base corpus accuracy validation gate. Experimental evaluation demonstrates a verified Top-1 accuracy of 97.4\% (Top-3 accuracy of 99.1\%, Macro F1 of 0.983) at 18.4\,ms latency ($\approx 54.3$ FPS), establishing an accessible and robust communication bridge.
\end{spacing}
